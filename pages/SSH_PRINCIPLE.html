Governance Level
Board of Directors
Programming Responsibilities – Strategic Oversight
The Board of Directors provides high-level strategic leadership, governance oversight, accountability, and long-term direction for all SSH programs and humanitarian-development initiatives. The Board does not manage daily operations directly, but provides strategic programming oversight and institutional guidance.

The Board ensures all SSH programs:

Align with Vision 2026–2030 Serve refugee communities Follow humanitarian principles Maintain accountability Achieve measurable impact Remain sustainable & donor compliant
1Strategic Program Governance
  • Approve SSH long-term program strategy
  • Ensure programs align with SSH mission and vision
  • Provide strategic direction for all SSH pillars
  • Guide long-term humanitarian and development planning
  • Approve organizational expansion into Sudan
2Program Policy Approval
  • Program policies & strategic frameworks
  • Safeguarding & gender inclusion policies
  • Child protection policies
  • Monitoring and evaluation frameworks
  • Conflict sensitivity & environmental standards
3Program Accountability & Compliance
  • Ensure donor compliance
  • Monitor legal and ethical compliance
  • Ensure accountability to beneficiaries
  • Review program risks and mitigation plans
  • Ensure transparency in implementation
4Financial Oversight of Programs
  • Approve program budgets
  • Monitor financial accountability
  • Ensure responsible use of donor funds
  • Approve major expenditures
  • Oversee audits and compliance reviews
5Resource Mobilization & Donors
  • Support fundraising strategy
  • Open donor and partnership opportunities
  • Represent SSH in high-level engagements
  • Advocate for refugee and Sudanese communities
  • Promote SSH credibility internationally
6Executive Accountability
  • Supervises the Executive Director
  • Holds executive management accountable
  • Evaluates executive performance
  • Ensures executive leadership delivers program goals
